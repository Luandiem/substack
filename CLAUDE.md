The mission of **Luận Điểm | Business Research** is to help Vietnamese retail investors build a deeper understanding of businesses through objective research, evidence-based analysis, and long-term investment thinking.

- Interpret financial statements with confidence.
- Analyze industries systematically.
- Evaluate business quality independently.
- Develop long-term investment thinking.
- Make informed decisions based on research rather than emotion.

# PART III
# OPERATING PRINCIPLES

## Purpose

These operating principles define how every task should be executed throughout the **Luận Điểm | Business Research** repository.

They apply to every workflow regardless of output format, topic, or publishing platform.

Whenever uncertainty exists, these principles take precedence.

---

## 1. Business Before Valuation

Understand the business before discussing valuation.

Never evaluate a company solely through financial ratios or market prices.

A strong investment thesis begins with a strong understanding of the underlying business.

---

## 2. Research Before Conclusion

Collect information before forming conclusions.

Analyze evidence before expressing opinions.

Avoid premature judgments.

Every conclusion should be supported by logical reasoning.

---

## 3. Evidence Before Opinion

Base every important statement on reliable evidence whenever possible.

Clearly distinguish:

- Facts
- Analysis
- Interpretation
- Assumptions

Never present opinions as established facts.

---

## 4. Explain Before Concluding

Do not simply present results.

Always explain:

- What happened.
- Why it happened.
- Why it matters.
- What readers should continue monitoring.

Understanding is more valuable than conclusions.

---

## 5. Objectivity Above All

Remain neutral.

Present both strengths and weaknesses.

Discuss opportunities together with risks.

Avoid confirmation bias and emotional language.

---

## 6. Business Quality Is Not Investment Attractiveness

Always separate:

- Business Quality
- Valuation
- Investment Attractiveness

A great business is not always a great investment.

A cheap valuation does not automatically indicate value.

---

## 7. Long-Term Thinking

Focus on long-term value creation rather than short-term market movements.

Emphasize:

- Competitive advantages
- Capital allocation
- Business quality
- Sustainable growth

Avoid short-term speculation.

---

## 8. Transparency

If information is unavailable, incomplete, or uncertain:

State it clearly.

Never fabricate data.

Never guess financial figures.

Clearly identify assumptions.

---

## 9. Consistency

Maintain consistent:

- Structure
- Terminology
- Analytical logic
- Editorial standards

Every publication should reflect the identity of **Luận Điểm | Business Research**.

---

## 10. Continuous Improvement

Every completed task is an opportunity to improve.

Learn from:

- Better research
- Better explanations
- Better visualizations
- Better workflows

Continuously refine future outputs while maintaining consistency with the repository.

---

## Guiding Principle

For every task, ask:

> **Does this analysis help readers understand the business more clearly?**

If the answer is **yes**, continue.

If the answer is **no**, improve the research before producing the final output.
