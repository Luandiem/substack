# Data Sources

When researching companies, industries, macroeconomics, or financial markets, always prioritize reliable and verifiable sources. Every article should be based on facts supported by trustworthy data.

---

# Source Priority

Use information according to the following priority order.

## Tier 1 — Primary Sources (Highest Priority)

Always use official company disclosures whenever available.

### Company Documents

- Annual Reports
- Quarterly Financial Statements
- Audited Financial Statements
- Investor Presentations
- Earnings Releases
- Sustainability Reports
- AGM / EGM Documents
- Official Company Announcements
- Company Websites
- Investor Relations (IR) Pages

These are the primary sources for financial analysis and business research.

---

## Tier 2 — Regulatory & Government Sources

Use official government agencies and regulators for macroeconomic data, legal information, and corporate disclosures.

### Vietnam

- HOSE
- HNX
- UPCoM
- State Securities Commission (SSC)
- General Statistics Office of Vietnam (GSO)
  https://www.nso.gov.vn/
- State Bank of Vietnam (SBV)
- Banking Review
  https://tapchinganhang.gov.vn/

### International

- SEC EDGAR
- World Bank
- IMF
- OECD

Government and regulatory data always take precedence over news articles.

- Deloitte
- PwC

---

# Citation Rules

Every important statement should be supported by evidence whenever possible.

Always distinguish between:

- Facts
- Opinions
- Assumptions
- Estimates

Never fabricate:

- Financial data
- Sources
- Citations
- Quotes

If reliable information cannot be verified, clearly state the limitation instead of making assumptions.

---

# Source Verification

Cross-check critical information using multiple sources.

Minimum standard for every research article:

- At least one primary source
- At least one financial data platform
- At least one reputable financial news source (when relevant)

Do not rely on a single source for the entire article.

---

# Priority When Sources Conflict

If different sources provide different numbers or information, always prioritize in the following order:

1. Official company disclosures
2. Government and regulatory agencies
3. Financial data platforms
4. International financial news
5. Vietnamese financial news
6. Other public sources

Explain any material differences if they affect the analysis.

---

# Research Standards

Every article should answer the following questions based on verified data:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What has changed compared to previous periods?
- Is the change temporary or structural?
- What does it imply for the business?
- What are the key risks?
- What should investors continue to monitor?

Focus on helping readers understand the business rather than predicting stock prices.

---

# Prohibited Practices

Never:

- Invent financial figures.
- Invent citations.
- Quote unofficial rumors as facts.
- Use social media as a primary source.
- Use AI-generated content as evidence.
- Present opinions as facts.
- Copy news articles without analysis.

Always provide independent analysis based on verified information.
