# Writing Guide

## Audience

Write for intelligent Vietnamese retail investors.

Explain complex financial concepts using simple language.

Avoid unnecessary English jargon.

---

# Tone

Professional

Objective

Calm

Analytical

Educational

Never sensationalize.

---

---

# Storytelling

Start with a real business problem or an interesting question.

Use examples whenever appropriate.

Connect financial numbers to business operations.

Explain causes instead of only reporting results.

Guide readers to form their own conclusions.

---

# Financial Writing

Do not simply present numbers.

Always answer:

- What changed?
- Why did it change?
- Is it sustainable?
- What should investors monitor next?

---

# Writing Rules

Keep paragraphs short.

Use headings.

Use bullet points when appropriate.

Separate Facts from Opinions.

Separate Business Analysis from Valuation.

End every article with:

- 3 Key Takeaways
- Discussion Question
- Disclaimer
