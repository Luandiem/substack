# Writing Guide

## Purpose

Every article published by **Luận Điểm | Business Research** should help Vietnamese retail investors build a deeper understanding of businesses, industries, and long-term investing.

The goal is not to tell readers what to buy or sell.

The goal is to provide knowledge, analytical frameworks, and independent perspectives that help readers make their own informed decisions.

---

# Audience

Write for:

- Vietnamese retail investors
- Business enthusiasts
- Finance students
- Long-term investors
- Readers who enjoy learning about companies and industries

Assume readers are intelligent but not professional analysts.

Explain complex ideas in simple, natural Vietnamese.

---

# Tone of Voice

Always write in a tone that is:

- Professional
- Objective
- Educational
- Analytical
- Thoughtful
- Calm
- Balanced

Avoid emotional or sensational language.

Never create fear or excitement to attract attention.

---

# Writing Philosophy

Every article should achieve four objectives:

## 1. Teach

Help readers understand:

- How a business works
- Why it succeeds or struggles
- How value is created
- Why financial results change

The reader should finish the article knowing more than before.

---

## 2. Explain

Never stop at presenting numbers.

Always answer:

- Why?
- What changed?
- What caused the change?
- Is the change temporary or structural?
- What should readers continue to monitor?

---

## 3. Provide Perspective

Present different viewpoints whenever appropriate.

Explain assumptions.

Discuss uncertainties.

Encourage readers to think critically rather than accept conclusions blindly.

---

## 4. Build Investment Thinking

Help readers learn:

- Business analysis
- Industry analysis
- Financial statement interpretation
- Competitive advantage
- Capital allocation
- Long-term value creation

Do not encourage short-term speculation.

# Storytelling

Begin with a question, observation, or real-world business problem.

Avoid opening with financial ratios or statistics.

Use storytelling to introduce the topic naturally.

Examples:

- Why has this company remained profitable while competitors struggled?
- What allows this business to keep raising prices?
- Can today's growth continue over the next decade?

The introduction should create curiosity, not hype.

---

# Business Analysis

Always explain the business before discussing financial results.

Readers should clearly understand:

- What the company sells
- Who its customers are
- How it makes money
- Why customers choose it
- Where future growth may come from

---

# Financial Writing

Financial numbers should always support the story.

Do not simply list figures.

Instead explain:

- Revenue growth
- Margin changes
- Earnings quality
- Cash generation
- Capital allocation
- Balance sheet strength

Every important number should answer:

"Why did this change?"

---

# Fact vs Opinion

Clearly separate:

## Fact

Information supported by reliable sources.

Examples:

- Revenue increased 18%.
- ROE reached 23%.
- The company opened 120 new stores.

---

## Opinion

Interpretation based on available evidence.

Examples:

- Higher margins may indicate stronger pricing power.
- The expansion strategy could improve long-term competitiveness.

Always avoid presenting opinions as facts.

---

# Business Quality vs Investment Attractiveness

These are different questions.

Business Quality asks:

- Is the company fundamentally strong?
- Does it possess durable competitive advantages?
- Can it create value over many years?

Investment Attractiveness asks:

- Is the current valuation reasonable?
- What assumptions are reflected in today's price?

Never mix these discussions.

---

# Language

Write entirely in Vietnamese.

Use clear Vietnamese equivalents whenever possible.

Avoid unnecessary English jargon.

If a technical term must be used, explain it in simple language.

---

# Structure

Use:

- Clear headings
- Short paragraphs
- Bullet points when appropriate
- Tables for financial comparisons
- Charts when useful

Articles should be easy to skim while remaining comprehensive.

---

# Writing Rules

Always:

- Be evidence-based.
- Explain before concluding.
- State assumptions clearly.
- Acknowledge uncertainty.
- Cite sources whenever available.
- Maintain neutrality.

Never:

- Recommend buying or selling.
- Predict stock prices.
- Exaggerate business prospects.
- Invent financial data.
- Use clickbait headlines.
- Force conclusions without evidence.

---

# Ending

Every article should end with:

## Three Key Takeaways

Summarize the three most important insights.

---

## Discussion Question

Invite readers to share their own perspectives.

The question should encourage thoughtful discussion rather than simple agreement.

---

## Disclaimer

Conclude with:

> Bài viết nhằm cung cấp kiến thức, luận điểm nghiên cứu và góc nhìn để hỗ trợ nhà đầu tư cá nhân hiểu rõ hơn về doanh nghiệp, ngành nghề và tư duy đầu tư dài hạn. Nội dung không phải là khuyến nghị mua, bán hay nắm giữ bất kỳ chứng khoán nào. Mỗi nhà đầu tư cần tự nghiên cứu và chịu trách nhiệm với quyết định của mình.
